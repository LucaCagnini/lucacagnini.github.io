About me

I hold an MSc in Bioinformatics from the University of Bologna and carried out my thesis research in the Endocrine Regulatory Genomics Lab at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcellona. During my academic journey, I also had the opportunity to gain research experence in Germany at Greifswald Universitat, Greifswald.

My interests range from the computational analysis of high-throughput data and the design and interpretation of clinical studies. I am trained in clinical research, including ICH GCP E6(R3), which lets me work in line with the standards clinical studies require. On the computational side, I worked with RNA-seq and neuroimaging data, developing various projects from Machine Learning to classical data analysis. I work in R, Python and Linux, often on HPC infrastructure.

I enjoy working from data management and quality control to statistical modelling, machine learning and multi-omics integration, and I am keen to collaborate on various projects where rigorous data analysis makes a difference.
