About me

I hold an MSc in Bioinformatics from the University of Bologna and carried out my thesis in the Endocrine Regulatory Genomics Lab at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona. During my studies, I also gained international research experience at the University of Greifswald, in Germany. Alongside my computational background, I am trained in clinical research regulation, including ICH GCP E6(R3) and Regulation (EU) No 536/2014, which lets me work in line with the standards that clinical studies require.

My interests range from the computational analysis of high-throughput data and its interpretation, to the entire process of a clinical study, starting from patient recruitment and submission to the ethics committee. I apply classical statistical analysis to clinical and non-clinical data, working in R, Python and Linux, often on HPC infrastructure.

I enjoy working across the whole analysis pipeline, from data management and quality control to statistical modelling, machine learning and multi-omics integration, and I am keen to collaborate on projects where rigorous data collection and analysis makes a difference.

Beyond work

Sport has been a constant in my life. I competed in track and field for ten years and I became a certified instructor of the Italian Athletics Federation (FIDAL) and coached groups of athletes at CUS Bologna. Along the way, I had the honour of carrying the Olympic flame during the torch relay for the Winter Olympics.

I also volunteer with Mera Dil ODV, an Italian volunteer organisation (Organizzazione di Volontariato) that supports disadvantaged children and communities in India. With them I took part in a teaching experience with children in India.
